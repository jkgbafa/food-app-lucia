Spiritual books reveal the invisible. They are the spirit of revelation — they make the invisible become visible and identifiable.

The Evil of Ignorance

“Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices.”2 Corinthians 2:11 KJV

Ignorant (adjective)

Lack of knowledge, education, information, or awareness — in general or about a particular thing.

Satan has the advantage when you are:

uninformed, uneducated, untrained, unaware, oblivious, untaught, asleep, unenlightened, unlearned, clueless, naive, inexperienced, unconscious, unfamiliar, unschooled, unskilled, senseless

You remove Satan's advantage when you are — about his devices:

knowledgeable, educated, informed, aware, alert, enlightened, intelligent, perceptive, wise, learned, understanding, conscious, awake

Advantage (noun)

A condition or circumstance that puts one in a favorable or superior position; a benefit or gain.

When you are ignorant about how Satan spoils your marriage, you are giving him:

the whip hand, supremacy, benefit, gain, edge, profit, leverage, the upper hand, an asset, the lead, privilege, superiority, a trump card, dominance, power, mastery, control, sway, the high ground, opportunity

Satan is able to get the advantage, the upper hand, and superiority over you when you are uninformed, uneducated, unaware and untaught about the way he operates and the way he destroys certain things. In John 10, Jesus shows us clearly that the devil comes to be a thief in our lives, to be a killer and to be a destroyer. But he doesn't do it by appearing in a mask or by dressing up as an armed robber. He does it through ignorance, and he does it through different devices. So if the devil will have the advantage in a marriage, according to Scripture, it would be through ignorance. Therefore it is important to fight Satan in my marriage by fighting ignorance in the person I'm married to — and it is of equal importance that I am not ignorant of his devices of how he unbuilds marriages.

Why is it important to teach my wife?

  • If you cannot lead your household, you cannot lead the church (1 Timothy 3:5). Therefore it's important to see how the church was led successfully — and a good example is the renowned, successful and anointed Apostle Paul. From this scripture we can glean that how you lead the church, God's bride, is how you should lead your wife. Prophet also mentioned this at the Use It or Lose It camp: you can directly apply the art-of-leadership principles into your marriage.
  • One way Apostle Paul led the church was by fighting ignorance. Eleven times throughout different letters he said he would not have the church ignorant about certain things. Therefore, to lead your wife the way you lead the church is to fight any ignorance that may exist in her.

11 Times Paul Fought Ignorance in the Church

01Paul exposed ignorant worship in Athens
“For as I passed by, and beheld your devotions, I found an altar with this inscription, TO THE UNKNOWN GOD. Whom therefore ye IGNORANTLY worship, him declare I unto you.”Acts 17:23 KJV

Paul confronted idol worship built on ignorance. He taught that true worship requires revelation, not mere tradition.

02Paul warned the Romans not to be ignorant of his role in their life
“Now I would not have you IGNORANT, brethren, that oftentimes I purposed to come unto you…”Romans 1:13 KJV

Paul wanted the believers to know his heart and purpose.

03Paul corrected ignorance about basic and foundational Christian truths
“For they being IGNORANT of God's righteousness, and going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God.”Romans 10:3 KJV

The Jews' ignorance caused them to reject grace. Paul revealed that righteousness is received, not achieved.

04Paul unveiled the mystery hidden from the ignorant
“For I would not, brethren, that ye should be IGNORANT of this mystery… that blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in.”Romans 11:25 KJV

He revealed God's secret plan for Israel and the Gentiles — showing that ignorance blinds believers to divine timing.

05Paul fought ignorance about Israel's spiritual history
“Moreover, brethren, I would not that ye should be IGNORANT, how that all our fathers were under the cloud…”1 Corinthians 10:1 KJV

He reminded the church not to repeat Israel's mistakes. Overcome the disadvantages of youthfulness by studying history.

06Paul corrected ignorance about spiritual gifts
“Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you IGNORANT.”1 Corinthians 12:1 KJV

Many believers operate gifts without understanding. Paul taught that revelation protects power from misuse.

07Paul warned against persistent ignorance
“But if any man be IGNORANT, let him be IGNORANT.”1 Corinthians 14:38 KJV

Paul showed that stubborn ignorance is a choice. Some reject truth even when it's clear, and remain spiritually dull.

08Paul fought ignorance about suffering in ministry
“For we would not, brethren, have you IGNORANT of our trouble which came to us in Asia…”2 Corinthians 1:8 KJV

He revealed his personal affliction to teach that ministry includes hardship. Ignorance about suffering produces weak Christians.

09Paul exposed ignorance of Satan's strategies
“Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not IGNORANT of his devices.”2 Corinthians 2:11 KJV

Spiritual maturity includes awareness. Paul warned that ignorance gives Satan advantage over believers.

10Paul comforted those ignorant about death
“But I would not have you to be IGNORANT, brethren, concerning them which are asleep…”1 Thessalonians 4:13 KJV

Ignorance about eternity leads to unnecessary sorrow. Paul replaced confusion with hope in the resurrection.

11Paul remembered his own ignorance before Christ
“Who was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious: but I obtained mercy, because I did it IGNORANTLY in unbelief.”1 Timothy 1:13 KJV

Paul admitted his past blindness. God's mercy transforms the ignorant persecutor into a wise teacher. Satan had the advantage over Paul because of his ignorance.
