Shopping in Accra

Ingredients

Special sauce

Method

  1. 01Heat the oven to 425°F and put a wire rack inside a sheet pan.
  2. 02Mix the special-sauce ingredients.
  3. 03Season and divide the beef into eight portions.
  4. 04Spread one portion thinly over half of each tortilla. Add cheese, fold and spray lightly with oil.
  5. 05Bake beef-side up for 7–9 minutes.
  6. 06Broil for 1–2 minutes per side until browned and crisp.
  7. 07Open carefully and add lettuce, onion, tomato, pickles and sauce.
