Method

  1. 01Roast the peanuts again at 400°F for 5 minutes.
  2. 02Blend until grainy, scrape down the sides and continue until creamy.
  3. 03Blend in salt and optional honey.
  4. 04Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3 weeks.
