Nashville Popcorn Chicken + Buttermilk Ranch

21 ingredients · 4 steps · Chicken · Dinner · Spicy

View original post

Note

Serve with rice or regular mashed potatoes; skip the suggested slaw.

Shopping in Accra

Buttermilk isn't sold in Accra — use 1 cup milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice, rested 10 minutes.

Buttermilk chicken

Breading

Spicy oil

Buttermilk ranch

Method

  1. 01Marinate the chicken as long as practical.
  2. 02Use separate wet and dry hands while breading.
  3. 03Double-fry for maximum crunch, or air-fry for 8–10 minutes, flipping halfway and spraying with oil.
  4. 04Toss or drizzle with the spiced oil and serve with ranch.
