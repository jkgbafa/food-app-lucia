Chocolate Moist Cake with Dark-Chocolate Crémeux

20 ingredients · 3 steps · Dessert

View original post

Cake

Dark-chocolate crémeux

Cream-cheese filling

Method

  1. 01Bake the cake for 30–35 minutes in a 13 × 10-inch tray.
  2. 02Cut it to fit a 4 × 8-inch loaf pan and slice the cake horizontally for more layers.
  3. 03Layer with crémeux and cream-cheese filling.
