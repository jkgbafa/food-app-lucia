Peanut-Butter Protein Cookies

Shopping in Accra

Protein powder is at supplement shops, not supermarkets. Swap PB2 for regular peanut butter.

Original version — 6 cookies

Lighter version — 6 cookies

Method

  1. 01Heat the oven to 160°C.
  2. 02Mash the banana and mix in the peanut butter and protein powder.
  3. 03Rest the mixture for 1–2 minutes.
  4. 04Form six cookies, flatten lightly and add optional chocolate chips.
  5. 05Bake for 10–12 minutes. Cool for 5 minutes before moving them.
